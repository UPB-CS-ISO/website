# 05. Users and Permissions

## You will learn

- what a **user** is for the kernel: a label (the **UID**) that every process runs under, and the UID ranges
- how the local users database (`/etc/passwd`) works and how programs read it with `getent`
- how passwords are stored locally: **hashes**, **salt** and why they live in `/etc/shadow`
- what **groups** are (`/etc/group`) and how they give rights to several users at once
- what happens when you **log in** and how a process gets the label of a user
- who the **`root`** user is and how to become `root` with **`sudo`** and **`su`**
- how **file permissions** work (`r`, `w`, `x` for files and directories) and how to change them with `chmod`, `umask`, `chown` and `chgrp`
- what the **special permissions** are: `setuid`, `setgid` and the sticky bit
- why you should not trust the **prompt** to tell you who you are
