# 05. Network

## You will learn

- a short history of the Internet and what **Internet services** are (the Web is one of them)
- what a **network card** and a **MAC address** are
- what an **Ethernet** and a **Wi-Fi** frame look like
- how **hubs** and **switches** connect computers in a local network, and why switches replaced hubs
- how to connect to a Wi-Fi network with `nmcli`
- what **IP addresses**, **network masks** and **routers** are
- what **ports**, **TCP** and **UDP** are
- what a **Wi-Fi access point** and a **home gateway** do
- what a computer needs to reach the Internet: an **IP address and mask**, a **default gateway** and a **DNS server**
- how **DHCP** configures all of these automatically
