# 05. Network Infrastructure

## You will learn

- a short history of the Internet and what **Internet services** are (the Web is one of them)
- what a **network card** and a **MAC address** are
- what an **Ethernet** and a **Wi-Fi** frame look like
- how **hubs** and **switches** connect computers in a local network, and why switches replaced hubs
- how to connect to a Wi-Fi network with `nmcli`
- what **IP addresses**, **network masks** and **IP packets** are, and how to test a computer with `ping`
- how **NAT** hides the private addresses of a home behind one public address
- how **routers** connect networks, and how to see them with `traceroute`
- how to **capture packets** with `tcpdump` and Wireshark
