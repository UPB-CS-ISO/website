# 08. Shell Scripting

## You will learn

- what a **shell script** is, how it runs (`./backup.sh` after `chmod +x`, or `bash backup.sh`) and what the
  **shebang** (`#!/bin/bash`) does
- how to define, change, delete and `export` **variables**, and why a variable always holds **text**
- how an unquoted `$var` is cut into words at the characters of **`IFS`**, and why you quote it: `"$var"`
- how to **read input** with `read` (`-p`, `-r`, several variables, `IFS=: read`)
- how to capture the output of a command with **command substitution**: `$(...)`
- how to compute: why `$a+$b` is just text, `expr`, **arithmetic** with `$((...))` and `((...))`, and decimals with
  `bc`
- how `if` **runs a command** as its condition, how `test` and `[ ... ]` (a program!) check conditions, and what
  `[[ ]]` and `(( ))` add
- how `case` compares a word with **patterns** (globs)
- how `while` and `until` repeat a command, and how `while read` goes over the lines of a file
- how `for` goes over **words** (a list, a glob, `$(...)`), how to count with a **step** (`seq`, `{1..10..3}`,
  `for ((...))`) and how `IFS` changes the list
- how to write **functions** with `local` variables, and give a result through the output (`$(...)`) and the exit
  code (`return`)
- how a script reads its **parameters**: `$0`, `$1`, ..., `$#` and `"$@"`
