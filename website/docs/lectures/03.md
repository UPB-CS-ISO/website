# 03. Processes

## You will learn

- the difference between an **executable** and a **process**
- how an executable is **loaded** into a process
- how processes are created with `fork` and `exec`
- the **states** of a process, and what **orphan** and **zombie** processes are
- how Linux starts and why `init` cannot stop
- what **file descriptors** are and how **redirects** work
- what **signals** are and how to handle them with `trap`
