# 03. Processes

## You will learn

- how applications are installed from **repositories** with `dnf` (Fedora) and `apt` (Ubuntu)
- the difference between an **executable** and a **process**
- how an executable is **loaded** into a process
- how processes are created with `fork` and `exec`
- the **states** of a process, and what **orphan** and **zombie** processes are
- how Linux starts and why `init` cannot stop
- what **file descriptors** are
- how **redirects** and **pipes** work, and the special files `/dev/null`, `/dev/zero` and `/dev/urandom`
- what **soft links** are, how they are followed and when they are broken
- how to look inside processes with the `/proc` folder
